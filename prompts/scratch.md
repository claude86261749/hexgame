# Task: draw one diagram for a live question

A reader is talking with a guide about a paper. The guide decided that no existing diagram fits the reader's question and asked you to draw a new one. You get the paper's digest, the guide's brief, the reader's question, the requested type, and relevant sections.

Draw exactly what the brief asks, at the size of the general diagrams. Keep it small: the guide will add orange highlights and notes to it while talking, using the ids you give elements, so give every element a short meaningful id. Use `nav` and `head` from the brief if given.
