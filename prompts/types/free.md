## Type: free

Hand-placed primitives on a 560-wide canvas (y grows downward; keep y under 360). Kinds: `box` (x, y, w, h, text), `text` (x, y baseline, text), `arrow` (x, y → x2, y2), `circle` (x, y centre, w diameter). `accent` colours an element; `muted` greys it. Give `id` and `note` to anything the reader can select. Leave 16 units of margin. Estimate text width as 6.5 units per character; boxes must be wider than their text plus 12.
Use this only when no other type can show the idea.
